# Sound credits

Five of the six bundled alarm tones are edited recordings from Wikimedia Commons, under CC BY 4.0,
CC BY-SA 4.0 or in the public domain. The sixth, the default tone `lollipop.mp3`, is synthesised by
`scripts/gen_tones.py` in this repository and licensed GPL-3.0-or-later like the app. The file
names are unchanged from earlier versions, so no code had to change.

| File | Title | Author | Source | Licence | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `lollipop.mp3` | Playful Chime | Crescendo Alarm contributors | generated by `scripts/gen_tones.py` in this repository | GPL-3.0-or-later | synthesised (marimba-like pentatonic phrase, 4.0 s seamless loop); encoded to MP3 192 kbit/s |
| `annoying_alarm.mp3` | Alarm clock ringing | Work With Sounds / Technical Museum of Slovenia | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WWS_Alarmclockringing.ogg> | CC-BY-4.0 | cut 5.0 s from the steady part (4.0-9.0 s); loop point crossfaded over 0.3 s; resampled to 44.1 kHz; loudness normalised; converted from Ogg Vorbis to MP3 192 kbit/s |
| `old_telephone_ring.mp3` | Automatic telephone Siemens & Halske - ringing | Work With Sounds / Technical Museum of Slovenia | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WWS_AutomatictelephoneSiemensampHalske8211ringing.ogg> | CC-BY-4.0 | two consecutive rings kept (from 10.04 s and 15.04 s), each cut to 3.0 s (pause shortened from about 4 s to about 2 s) with a 0.5 s fade-out; mono to stereo; resampled to 44.1 kHz; loudness normalised; converted from Ogg Vorbis to MP3 192 kbit/s |
| `wake_up.mp3` | Beep alarm clock | Jeuwre | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beep_alarm_clock.ogg> | CC-BY-SA-4.0 | trimmed to five whole beep-beep periods (0.27-5.41 s), leading silence removed; mono to stereo; resampled to 44.1 kHz; loudness normalised; converted from Ogg Vorbis to MP3 192 kbit/s |
| `wakeywakey.mp3` | Rooster crowing | Filo gèn' | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rooster_crowing.ogg> | CC-BY-SA-4.0 | leading silence removed (from 0.99 s), crow tail faded out, padded with silence to 3.5 s; resampled to 44.1 kHz; loudness normalised; converted from Ogg Vorbis to MP3 192 kbit/s |
| `wakeywakey2.mp3` | WWII submarine dive klaxon | United States Navy | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WWII_submarine_dive_klaxon.ogg> | LicenseRef-PublicDomain | three whole klaxon cycles kept (0.94-3.77 s); mono to stereo; upsampled from 11.025 kHz to 44.1 kHz; loudness normalised; converted from Ogg Vorbis to MP3 192 kbit/s |

"Loudness normalised" means a linear gain to about -10 LUFS integrated, capped so the true peak
stays below -1.5 dBTP before encoding. No compression or limiting was applied. Each file's ID3 tags
carry its title, author, licence and source page.

The two CC BY-SA 4.0 files (`wake_up.mp3`, `wakeywakey.mp3`) are edited, so they are adaptations.
They stay under CC BY-SA 4.0, not the app's GPL. Anyone reusing them has to credit the author named
above and share their own changes under CC BY-SA 4.0. CC BY-SA 4.0 is one-way compatible with GPLv3,
so the files can ship inside the app. The CC BY 4.0 files also require the credit above.
`wakeywakey2.mp3` is a work of the U.S. federal government (`PD-USGov-Military-Navy` on its Commons
page) and is in the public domain, so the credit for it is a courtesy. Filo gèn' also offers
`Rooster_crowing.ogg` under the GFDL and earlier CC BY-SA versions; this file uses CC BY-SA 4.0.

## History

- **Until 2026-09-17** the six files had no recorded source, and inspection showed they were rips.
  One carried YouTube-to-MP3 tags (`publisher=__KIKO__`, `comment=Rate And Subscribe`). Two came from
  a fan remix of an internet meme that traces back to a private family video. The other three had
  no metadata at all (`docs/TODO.md` T-29).
- **From 2026-09-17 to 2026-10-01** they were replaced by six Mixkit previews under the Mixkit Sound
  Effects Free License. That licence is not free: it forbids redistributing an item on its own, and
  Mixkit's terms forbid making items available to third parties. A public source repository and an
  F-Droid source tarball both do that.
- **On 2026-10-01** the Mixkit files were replaced by the free files above (`docs/TODO.md` T-212).
  The earlier files remain in the git history, which was not rewritten.
