# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the Crescendo Alarm app ("the App") collects, uses, and protects
your personal data.

## Data Collected

The App collects the following data, all of it stored locally on your device:

- Calendar Data: With your permission, the App reads your calendar to derive intelligent wake-up
  times based on your commitments. The App does not write to or modify your calendar.

- Camera Data: With your permission, the App uses your camera to scan a QR code or barcode - to
  import it as your deactivation code, or to check it when a "guaranteed wake-up" alarm rings. The
  camera feed is used only at the moment of scanning to detect the code - no photo or video is
  stored or transmitted.

- Alarms and Sleep-Habit Settings: alarms you create, your sleep-habit preferences, which calendars
  and events you chose to use or ignore, any alarm tones you import (copied into the App's own
  storage), and a deactivation code with an optional note describing it (if you choose to set one
  up - generated by the App or imported from a code you scan) are stored on your device to make the
  App work. Two small pieces of this - the time of your next alarm and, if you use Do Not Disturb
  (below), the start and end of your sleep window - are additionally kept in the part of your
  device's storage that Android makes readable before you first unlock the phone after a restart.
  That is deliberate: it lets the App sound a fallback alarm and switch Do Not Disturb on and off
  while the phone is still locked. It never leaves your device.

- Do Not Disturb: only if you switch it on under Sleep Habits (it is off by default) and grant the
  App "Do Not Disturb access" in your phone's settings, the App switches your phone to "alarms
  only" during your sleep window and back off when your alarm rings. On Android 14 and older this
  is your phone's normal Do Not Disturb setting; on newer versions it is a mode belonging to the
  App. The App does not read or store anything about your other notifications.

- Notifications: the App schedules alarm notifications and, if enabled, a bedtime reminder. Their
  content is created by the App on your device.

- Diagnostics Log: only if you switch it on under Settings > Diagnostics (it is off by default), the
  App keeps a short technical record of what its alarm scheduler did - for example how many days it
  planned, whether a calendar read succeeded, and how many alarms it registered. It is stored on your
  device only, is limited to the most recent 512 entries in each of its two parts (one for the App
  itself, one for its background task), and can be switched off again,
  viewed and deleted there. By default it
  deliberately contains **no** wake-up times, no dates or times of day, no calendar content, no
  calendar or account names, and not your deactivation code: the code that writes this record has
  no way to store text at all, only counters and fixed categories. A separate, off-by-default
  switch under Settings > Diagnostics lets you additionally include exact clock times (minute of
  day, with no date attached), intended only for attaching to a bug report when a problem
  specifically needs it: each planned day's wake-up time and its earliest appointment, and the
  start and end time of every calendar event in the app's planning window - never its title,
  description, attendees, location, or which of your calendars it came from, whether or not this
  switch is on. Turning the switch on means the exported log does contain clock times, which
  together are a sleep pattern and a daily routine, so it should only be shared deliberately.
  Nothing is ever transmitted automatically - the only way any of this leaves your device is if you
  copy it yourself from that screen and choose to send it somewhere.

The App does not collect your name, email address, contact information, or your device's location.

The released App (the APK attached to each GitHub Release) makes no network requests of any kind
and declares no internet-access permission at all - the App is fully usable offline and never sends
data anywhere by itself. The only ways data leaves your device are ones you start yourself: the
"Share" and "Print" actions for your deactivation code hand it to an app or printer you pick, and
you can copy the diagnostics log as described above. Development (debug) builds additionally
declare that permission, as every Flutter debug build does, only so developer tools can connect to
the running app; the App's own code makes no network requests in them either.

## Use of Data

The collected data is stored locally on your device and is not shared with third parties. It is
used as follows:

- Calendar Data is used to intelligently adjust your wake-up times based on your scheduled events.

- Camera Data is used only to read a scanned code: to store it as your deactivation code, or to
  check it against the stored one.

- Alarms and Sleep-Habit Settings are used to run the App's core alarm and scheduling features.

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against
accidental or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, or damage.

## Partners

We do not share your data with anyone. We do not sell your data. The App makes no network requests
of its own; it may bundle third-party libraries to implement certain features, which may have their
own privacy policies we do not control.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting
the new Privacy Policy in the App. We recommend that you review this Privacy Policy periodically for
any updates.
