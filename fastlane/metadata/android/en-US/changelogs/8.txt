New in 1.7.0:
- Android 8.0 or newer is now required (it was 7.0); the app no longer ships a GPL-2.0 desugaring library.
- Licence: GPL-3.0-or-later; missing third-party notices (libyuv, dmfs) added.
- Settings > Alarm Screen, a one-time offer and a warning explain how the alarm screen opens by itself.
- Other apps can no longer make this one post notifications.
- Ring ends after 60 minutes; manual alarms follow the time zone when you open the app.
